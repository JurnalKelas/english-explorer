📜 Game Rules: English Explorer

  1. Lobby & Preparation Phase: The teacher inputs the roster of participating students into the student list box. The system automatically divides questions into randomized Question Packets.
  2. Spinner & Turn Mechanism: Click the SPIN button to randomly select a student and assign their specific Question Packet number.
  3. Gameplay & Answering Rules: The active student answers questions while the system automatically reads questions aloud via Voice Over.
  4. Scoring System: Each correct answer awards +10 points. Incorrect answers award 0 points with no deductions.
  5. Classroom Etiquette: Audience members are strictly prohibited from shouting out answers or spoiling the game for the active player.
  6. Hall of Fame: Final scores are automatically recorded and ranked in the leaderboard.
